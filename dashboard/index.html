How implied value is computed

implied = round( TVL × family multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × new-protocol penalty × breadth multiplier )

TVL and seven-day growth come from the snapshot. Age is the time since the protocol was first listed — the best record of how long it has run with no token.

Implied values are this entry’s own claim. They are computed from the snapshot figures by the one stated formula above. They are not prices, not predictions, and not investment advice. Snapshot source and date are shown at the top of the page.
